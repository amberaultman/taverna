Dining

Make us your home for special events

Book our group dining room and we’ll make sure you and your guests are well fed, whatever the occasion.

Dine with your guests in an elegant private setting and enjoy a dedicated service team and menu packages for every price range. Our group dining room is equipped with a 70-inch flat screen TV, WIFI access, Blue Ray, Apple TV and a state of the art audio system. A rustic floor-to-ceiling barn door separates the room from the rest of the restaurant and gives you a truly exclusive dining experience. In addition, we offer customized floor plans and seating to suit the style of your event.
